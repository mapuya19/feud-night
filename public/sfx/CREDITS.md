They retain that third-party license, not the repository's MIT license. Do not
redistribute them as standalone audio assets; review Mixkit's current terms

The sound effects were trimmed of silence, loudness-normalized to −16 LUFS,
and re-encoded as small MP3s.

`lobby-loop.wav` is an original procedural synth loop, generated by
`scripts/generate-lobby-music.mjs`, with no samples or third-party composition.
The generator and its output use the repository's MIT license. Regenerate it
with `node scripts/generate-lobby-music.mjs`.

The previous Mixkit stock-music excerpt was removed: the Stock Music Free
License, unlike the sound-effects license, excludes video games. See
https://mixkit.co/license/modal/musicFree/.

To swap sounds, update the files in `public/sfx/` and check their licenses.
See `src/lib/sfx.ts` for playback and toggles.
